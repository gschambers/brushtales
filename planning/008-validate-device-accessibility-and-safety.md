# 008 — Validate devices, accessibility, and safety

- **Status:** todo
- **Priority:** high
- **Sequence:** 8
- **Labels:** accessibility, qa, safety, release
- **Depends on:** 004, 006, 007

## Goal

Validate the complete supervised experience on representative iOS and Android devices before calling v0 ready for pilot use.

## Acceptance criteria

- A full two-minute session works on a recent iOS device and Android device in normal bathroom-like conditions.
- Permission denial, poor lighting, device rotation, audio interruption, and app backgrounding have clear recovery paths.
- Audio-first use is understandable with minimal screen attention; captions/transcripts are available to caregivers.
- Touch targets, contrast, text scaling, reduced motion, and volume behavior are reviewed.
- The test report includes known limitations and does not claim dental accuracy.
