## Goal

Write one original, audio-first fantasy story that fits a two-minute brushing session, with explicit decision points and at least two coherent outcomes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Story data is represented as typed, data-driven scenes and choices.
- The experience includes start, four approximate 30-second beats, decision prompts, and completion.
- Choices are understandable without reading the screen.
- Low-progress, high-progress, and camera-free paths all end positively without shame.
- Every narration clip has a script ID, transcript, duration target, and pronunciation notes.

Use `research/2026-09-12-interactive-audio-story-research.md` and `research/2026-09-12-tts-research.md` as inputs. The first story should be polished and short rather than procedurally generated.
