# 0006 — Use a deterministic authored story graph

**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Status:** Accepted for v0

## Context

Children should feel that brushing shapes an adventure, but child-facing
runtime-generated stories add moderation, consistency, latency, and privacy
risks.

## Decision

Author one local story graph with 3–4 choice points and 3–4 safe endings.
Choices and coarse session bands select reviewed clips and branches. Every path
must be satisfying; brushing performance changes opportunities or tone rather
than access to affection or a “good” child outcome.

## Alternatives considered

- Runtime AI-generated story text and audio.
- A linear audiobook with no choices.
- A reward/collection game with story as decoration.

## Consequences

Content production is required up front, but behavior is deterministic,
offline, testable, and reviewable by caregivers.
