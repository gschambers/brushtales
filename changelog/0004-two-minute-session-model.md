# 0004 — Use a two-minute, twice-daily routine model

**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The desired habit is brushing thoroughly and consistently for two minutes,
twice per day.

## Decision

Use a two-minute default session with pause/resume and optional reminders for
morning/evening. Do not punish missed sessions, extra sessions, or a camera
failure. The story should complete safely even when the child brushes without
camera confidence.

## Alternatives considered

- Require exactly two completed sessions per day.
- Use a variable timer based on age.
- Make the timer shorter for younger children.

## Consequences

Age adaptation belongs in wording and story pacing, not a misleading claim
that a shorter session is equivalent. Caregivers can supervise the routine.
