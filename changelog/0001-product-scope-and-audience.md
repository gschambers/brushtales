# 0001 — Define v0 audience and product scope

**Date:** 2026-09-19
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

The intended household includes children aged 4, 7, and 9. The product needs
to be useful across that range without becoming three separate apps.

## Decision

Target ages approximately 4–9 through local profile age bands that change
language, pacing, choice complexity, and visual scaffolding. Keep v0 to one
story, two-minute sessions, twice-daily routine support, local profiles, and
on-device persistence.

## Alternatives considered

- Target only preschool children and defer older readers.
- Build separate content/products for 4–5, 6–7, and 8–9.
- Use one identical narrative presentation for all ages.

## Consequences

Story content must be authored with multiple presentation levels. Profile data
needs an age-band field, but it must not be used to infer health or identity.
