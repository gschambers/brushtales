# Sky Reef — approved narration script

## Production notes

- Audience: ages 4–9, with the app selecting age-band choice copy.
- Voice: one warm adult narrator; the Sky Reef Guide may use a slightly brighter
  variation of the same adult voice.
- Never imply that a child earned care, food, affection, or safety through
  brushing. Every ending is welcoming.
- `[pause 500ms]` is an intentional breathing space. Do not remove it during
  synthesis.
- Pronounce **Auralume** as “OR-uh-loom.” It is an invented place name.

## Clips

### intro — narrator

Welcome to Sky Reef, where clouds drift like boats and the stars hum softly.
[pause 500ms] Our little crew is ready to explore. Keep brushing gently while
we follow the light.

### choice-1 — narrator prompt

Which way should we go? Follow the glowing bubbles, or chase the silver clouds?

### choice-bubbles — narrator choice label

Follow the glowing bubbles.

### choice-clouds — narrator choice label

Chase the silver clouds.

### bubbles-intro — sky-reef-guide

The bubbles lift us toward Auralume — OR-uh-loom — and paint a twinkly map in
the air. [pause 500ms] Something kind is waiting ahead.

### clouds-intro — sky-reef-guide

The silver clouds curl into a soft staircase. Up we go, one easy step at a
time, until a sleepy starfish navigator waves hello.

### choice-2 — narrator prompt

How should we wake the navigator? Ring the sky bell, or hum a brave song?

### choice-bell — narrator choice label

Ring the sky bell.

### choice-song — narrator choice label

Hum a brave song.

### bell-intro — sky-reef-guide

Ding-a-ling! The sky bell makes a tiny rainbow. The navigator smiles and points
to a bright reef trail.

### song-intro — sky-reef-guide

Hmm-hmm-hmm. Your gentle song floats across the reef. The navigator wakes with
 a happy wiggle and points to a bright reef trail.

### choice-3 — narrator prompt

The reef has found a pocket of starlight. Should we share it, or save a piece
for the journey?

### choice-share — narrator choice label

Share the starlight.

### choice-save — narrator choice label

Save a piece for the journey.

### ending-share — narrator positive ending

The crew shares the starlight, and the whole Sky Reef glows. Everyone has a
place in this adventure. When you are ready, we can explore again.

### ending-save — narrator positive ending

The crew saves one little star for the journey and leaves the rest shining for
the reef. Every explorer helped in their own way. When you are ready, we can
explore again.

### ending-bubbles — narrator positive fallback ending

The bubble map leads us home beneath a warm moon. The Sky Reef gives a friendly
wave, and the adventure rests until next time. You kept exploring with us.
